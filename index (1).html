FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

 

Do I need Microsoft Excel?

No. The planner works in Google Sheets at no cost, and also in Microsoft Excel.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. Open it in the Google Sheets app and update it from anywhere.

How long does setup take?

About ten minutes. Sample figures show you how everything works. Replace them with your own and you are ready.

Which currency does it use?

Amounts display in US dollars ($) by default, and the calculations work the same for any currency.
